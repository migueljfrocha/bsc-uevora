# General Physics I

Laboratory reports developed for the General Physics I course,
part of the Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science Engineering
at Universidade de Évora.

## Reports

| Report | Topic | Description | Type |
|--------|-------|-------------|------|

| [Lab Report&nbsp;2](./lab-report-2.pdf) | Rectilinear motion with constant acceleration | Study of motion data, experimental tables, graph analysis, and calculations for uniformly accelerated movement. | Laboratory |
| [Lab Report&nbsp;3](./lab-report-3.pdf) | Dynamic behavior of an elastic spring | Experimental study of an elastic spring, including data collection, graph analysis, and calculations. | Laboratory |

| [Lab Report&nbsp;5](./lab-report-5.pdf) | Specific heat capacity of solids | Calorimetry experiment for determining the specific heat capacity of solid materials. | Laboratory |
