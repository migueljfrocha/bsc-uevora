# Entrepreneurship and Innovation

A final entrepreneurship report developed for the Entrepreneurship and Innovation course, part of the Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science Engineering at Universidade de Évora.

## About

This project follows the development of a business idea from individual pitch proposals to a final group concept. The report includes the reasoning behind the selected idea, its business model, estimated costs, growth projections, and market entry strategy.

The work also documents the main difficulties found while developing the business concept.

## Main Topics

- Individual business pitch ideas
- Final business idea selection
- Business Model Canvas
- Cost structure
- Three-year growth projections
- Marketing strategy
- SWOT analysis
- Team contribution and lessons learned
