# Fileless Malware: A Review and Analysis of Detection Methods

A research paper and review article developed for the **Introduction to Research** course at Universidade de Évora.

## About

This project consists of a comprehensive review article focused on **Fileless Malware** - a sophisticated cybersecurity threat that operates within a system's memory without leaving traditional traces on the disk.

With the constant evolution of technology, threats to both individuals and organizations have become more complex. This paper analyzes the impact of fileless malware on the cybersecurity economy and explores its specific characteristics, distinguishing it from traditional file-based malware.
